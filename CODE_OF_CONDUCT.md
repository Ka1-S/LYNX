# Code of Conduct

LYNX is made by and for people who love making music. We want the issues, pull requests and every other LYNX space to be friendly and welcoming for everyone, whatever their experience, background or the DAW they come from.

## Expected
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to music software or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## Not accepted
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Posting cracked software, license keys, or links to pirated plugins and sample packs.
- Spam and self-promotion that has nothing to do with the discussion.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, lock threads and block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, use [GitHub's report content feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

This code is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
