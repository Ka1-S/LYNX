## Description [≤ 10,000 characters]

LYNX (published here as LYNX SHARE) is a free, open-source digital audio workstation for making beats and songs. It follows the pattern-based
workflow loved by beatmakers — a step-sequencer Channel Rack, patterns, a Piano Roll, a Playlist with arrangements
and a Mixer with inserts, sends and sidechain — and it is built to stay light and fast on any PC.

• Lightweight: a lock-free audio engine that spreads channels and mixer tracks across your CPU cores, skips idle
  channels and streams long audio from disk.
• Complete: more than 100 built-in instruments and effects — synths, drum machines, samplers, EQs, compressors,
  limiters, reverbs, delays, pitch correction, visualizers and more.
• Your plugins: hosts VST3, CLAP and VST2 instruments and effects, each optionally in its own sandbox process so a
  crashing plugin cannot take your session down.
• Opens FL Studio projects (.flp) with their patterns, notes, mixer, automation and samples, and imports MIDI files.
• Low latency: ASIO (including LYNX's own ASIO driver for any device), WASAPI shared and exclusive modes.
• Record audio and MIDI, edit audio, automate anything, render to WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG or stems.
• English and Brazilian Portuguese.

No account, no subscription, no ads and no telemetry. LYNX is free software (GNU AGPL-3.0); the source code is on
GitHub.

LYNX is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Image-Line. FL Studio is a trademark of Image-Line
Software. Other product names are trademarks of their respective owners.

LYNX is in alpha: expect rough edges, and please report problems on GitHub.
